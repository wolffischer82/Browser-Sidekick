Home heating grant 2026: who can apply

Grants cover up to 40 % of the cost of replacing a fossil-fuel heating system.

Updated 3 October 2026

Owners of homes built before 2010 can claim a grant of 30 % of eligible costs when they replace an oil or gas heating system with a heat pump. A further 10 % applies when the old boiler is more than 20 years old.

The grant is capped at 21,000 of eligible costs per home and must be applied for before work begins. An energy assessment by a certified adviser is required.

Applications for 2026 close on 15 December.
